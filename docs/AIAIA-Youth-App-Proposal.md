# The App at a Glance

## Why we are building this

Today our youth records, dues and event details are kept **on paper**, spread across more than 30 branches. This app brings it all into **one place, on everyone's phone**, and connects around **600 Anglo-Indian youth across India**. It is built to grow to 10,000 members, and eventually to serve the whole of AIAIA.

## What members will be able to do

- **Join and keep a profile:** name, branch, photo, city, studies or work. Adding an email is optional. Emails always come from AIAIA's official address.
- **Find each other:** a member directory, where each member chooses what others can see.
- **Stay informed:** announcements, with alerts on Android phones, on computers, and on iPhones that add the web app to their home screen.
- **Events:** see what's coming up and register yourself or your children. You get reminders.
- **Delegate pass & digital member ID:** a personal QR code, scanned at events and verifiable by **any branch**. Useful when you move to another state for studies or work, and you can request a **branch transfer** in the app. It can be **printed** for anyone without a phone.
- **Pay:** dues, event and NYM fees, and donations by UPI, card or net-banking, or **in cash** to your branch treasurer or the NYM committee at the venue. **Receipts are sent automatically** in the app, on WhatsApp if you opted in, and by email if you added one. Online payments also get a confirmation email from Razorpay.
- **Certificates:** a "My Certificates" tab where you can download every certificate you've earned. Each one has a QR code that proves it is genuine.
- **Gallery & *Review* archive:** curated photos and videos from our events, and past issues of the magazine.
- **Jobs, quizzes, polls and mentorship:** coming after launch.
- **Report a problem:** anyone can report something worrying, and a committee reviews it.

## How joining works: the "waiting room"

1. **Enter your mobile number** and type in the one-time code sent on WhatsApp or SMS. There are **no passwords**.
2. **Fill in your name, branch and basic details.**
3. **Wait in the waiting room** while your branch officers and the National Youth Committee are alerted.
4. **Get approved.** Only then does the full app open.
5. If you are already on our paper lists, approval is quick.
6. **People aged 35 and over** join as **AIAIA members** (the parent organisation) rather than the youth wing. Members also move across automatically on their 35th birthday.

## Who looks after what

| Who | What they can do |
|---|---|
| **App creators** (super admins) | Keep the app running and set everything up. The first time they use a new phone or laptop, they confirm a code sent **to their phone** and one sent **to their email**. After that, the device **stays signed in** and they're never asked again on it |
| **Mentor** (Governing Body) | See everything and step in on any decision. Can **ask** for someone to be blocked; the Enforcement Committee or a super admin decides |
| **National Youth Committee** | Approve members anywhere, post national news and events, see all payments, appoint branch officers, issue certificates |
| **Branch officers** | Approve their own branch's members, run branch events, record cash payments, and verify any member's digital ID |
| **Event teams** (e.g. the NYM committee) | For one event: scan passes, collect fees at the venue, and a **safety lead** who looks after minors |
| **Voluntary Enforcement Committee** | Review reports and **block** people who break the rules. Only they and the super admins can block anyone |
| **Support staff** | Help members who change their phone number by moving their account to the new number |
| **Members** | Use the app |

**Every admin action is recorded.** When the committee changes each term, access passes to the new office-bearers.

## Children and parents

- **18 and over:** join normally.
- **14 to 17 with their own phone:** join normally. When approving, the branch officer confirms that a parent has agreed. Their phone number and date of birth are never shown to other members.
- **Under 14, or without a phone:** a **parent or guardian (aged 18 or over)** adds the child under their own account. The parent registers them for events, pays, and receives their certificates and delegate pass.
- **Alerts for parents:** whenever a child under 18 **arrives** at an event venue, their parent or guardian gets a WhatsApp alert, or an app alert if they're a member. Each event's safety lead has a list of the minors attending and their parents' contacts.
- **Turning 18:** on their 18th birthday the account passes fully to the member. The parent's access and alerts end, and the member is asked to give **their own consent** the next time they open the app. Nothing about them becomes more visible until they choose.

## Event check-in

Every member has a **delegate pass**: a QR code on their phone, or printed on paper.
- **Scanning:** at the venue, the event team **scans each person's pass once, on arrival**, and checks the photo and name against the person in front of them. This makes it hard for someone to claim they attended when they didn't.
- **No internet needed:** scans are stored on the scanner's phone and uploaded once there is signal. Parents' alerts go out at that point, showing the real time of the scan.
- **Paying at the venue:** the same scan lets the team collect a fee in cash. The receipt reaches the member automatically.

## Certificates: how admins use them

1. A designer makes the certificate in Canva or any other tool and saves it as a PDF.
2. In the app, an admin **marks where the name, event, award and date go**.
3. After an event, the admin marks who took part and who won. Certificates appear **instantly** in each member's app.
4. Admins can see and download the full list of winners for any event.

## Official AIAIA WhatsApp updates

Admins can send important updates **straight to members' WhatsApp** from a verified **"AIAIA"** account:
- Members agree to receive these when they join, and can reply STOP at any time.
- Every message needs **a second person to approve it** before it goes out.
- Routine reminders go out as free app notifications instead, to keep costs low.

## Keeping data safe

- **Where data is kept:** member records and all photos, files and backups are **stored in India**, in Mumbai and Bangalore.
- **What passes through services outside India:** a few necessary services handle small amounts of data abroad. This is allowed under India's data protection law, and it is listed in our privacy policy:
  - **WhatsApp:** phone numbers and message text.
  - **Phone notification services:** notification text.
  - **Crash reports:** these contain no personal details.
  - **Email:** goes through AIAIA's own email provider. If that is Google or Microsoft, their servers may be outside India.
- **Who sees what:** only **approved members** see member information, and officers handle only their own branch.
- **Backups** are **locked with a key** that only the app owner holds. They are kept for at most 6 months.
- **Deleting an account:** members can see, correct or delete their data. Rejected applicants and deleted accounts are **kept for 6 months**, then permanently removed. Payment records are kept as long as our accountant advises.
- **Open source:** the app's code is open for anyone to read. Member data, passwords and keys are **never** part of it.

## What we need from AIAIA

- **Permission** for the youth app to operate under AIAIA's registration, with its registration documents, PAN and a bank account.
- A **D-U-N-S number** for AIAIA. It is free, takes about 2–4 weeks, and is needed to publish on Google Play.
- **Verification of AIAIA's official WhatsApp** business account.
- Details for our website's policy pages, which Razorpay and Google require:
  - registered address, phone and email
  - refund rules for dues, event fees and donations
  - dues amounts
- **Names** of:
  - the Enforcement Committee
  - support staff
  - a privacy contact (Grievance Officer)
  - treasurers
  - each event's team and safety lead
- **Certificate designs**, logos and signatures.
- The curated **photos and videos from the *Review* magazine**, and past issues.
- The existing **paper member lists**.
- The **official branch list**.
- A **parent consent form** with the parent's WhatsApp number and a tick-box for arrival alerts.

## Decisions the committee needs to make

1. **Where payment money goes:** one central AIAIA account, or each branch's own account.
2. **Refund rules** for dues, event fees and donations.
3. **Open-source licence:** must people who copy our code keep their version open too? *Recommended: yes.*
4. **Legal check:** a lawyer reviews the consent process for 14–17 year olds, the privacy policy and the terms.
5. **How long payment records are kept**, as advised by AIAIA's accountant.
6. **iPhone app:** sponsor, Apple waiver, or a budget increase.
