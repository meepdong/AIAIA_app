One app to bring every Anglo-Indian youth in India together
All India Anglo-Indian Association · Youth Wing

November 2026

Today

  • Member records are on paper, spread across 30+ branches.
  • Dues and event fees are tracked by hand. Receipts get lost.
  • Registrations and updates are scattered across many WhatsApp groups.
  • A member who moves cities can't easily prove membership to a new branch.
  • Certificates and event photos sit in folders and old magazines.

The idea

One app, on Android phones and any web browser. You join with just your mobile number; there are no passwords. Your branch approves you, and then everything is in one place: members, events, payments, certificates, photos and official news.

It's built for our youth first, with safety for under-18s built in, and it's ready to grow to serve the whole of AIAIA.

What members get

1

Join & get approved

A phone code, a short profile, then a waiting room until your branch approves you. Paper lists are loaded in, so existing members are approved quickly.

2

Member directory

Find each other by name, branch, city or profession. Every member chooses what others can see.

3

Events & delegate pass

Register yourself and your children, and get reminders. A QR pass, which can be printed, is scanned at the door, even without internet.

4

Digital member ID

Any branch can verify it. Moving for studies or work? Request a branch transfer in the app.

5

Payments & receipts

Pay dues, fees and donations by UPI or card, or in cash at your branch or the NYM desk. Receipts are sent automatically.

6

Certificates

They appear in your app straight after an event. Download them any time; a QR proves each one is genuine.

7

Gallery & Review archive

Curated event photos and videos, plus past issues of the Review magazine, for members only.

8

Official updates

News from the verified "AIAIA" WhatsApp account, plus free app alerts. Jobs, quizzes, polls and mentorship come after launch.

Safe, simple and affordable

The full details are in the separate Technical Sheet and Proposal.

Built safe for young members

  • Ages 14–17: a parent's agreement is confirmed by the branch. Under 14: children join through a parent's account.
  • Parents get a WhatsApp alert when their child arrives at an event.
  • Each event has a safety lead, with a list of the minors attending and their parents' contacts.
  • A minor's phone number and birthday are never shown to other members.
  • At 18, the account passes to the member, who gives their own consent. Parent access ends.
  • Anyone can report a concern. A Voluntary Enforcement Committee acts on it.

Your data, protected

  • Member records, photos and backups are stored in India (Mumbai and Bangalore).
  • Only approved members see member information. Officers handle only their own branch.
  • Every admin action is recorded. Only a committee can block an account.
  • Members can see, correct or delete their data. Closed accounts are removed after 6 months.
  • The code is open source; member data and keys are never in it.

What we need from AIAIA

  • Permission to run under AIAIA's registration (documents, PAN, bank account).
  • A free D-U-N-S number (for Google Play) and WhatsApp business verification.
  • Policy details (address, refund rules, dues) and the names of the people who will hold each role.
  • Certificate designs, Review photos and videos, paper member lists, the official branch list.
