# Privacy Policy

This policy is a placeholder draft, pending approval by AIAIA. It explains what the AIAIA Youth App collects, why, where it is kept, and the choices you have.

## What we collect

- **Identity and profile:** your name, branch, gender, photo, city, occupation, education and bio.
- **Contact and private details:** your phone number, date of birth, email address and emergency contact. These are kept apart from your profile (see "Who can see your private details" below).
- **Membership records:** your status, member number, roles, approvals and branch transfers.
- **Activity:** event RSVPs, check-ins, certificates, reports you file and notifications sent to you.
- **Payments:** dues, event fees and donations, with receipts. Card and UPI details are handled by Razorpay. We never see or store them.
- **Device data:** push notification tokens, and basic technical information needed to keep the app working.

## Who can see your private details

- **You** can always see and correct your own details.
- **AIAIA officers** can see every member's phone number, date of birth, email address and emergency contact, in every branch, including members under 18. Officers are AIAIA officials, such as branch and national office-bearers, and need these details to run membership and keep members safe. Officers can only read these details in the app. They cannot change them.
- **Guardians** can see and update the details of a child they are linked to, while the link is active.
- **Other members** never see your phone number or date of birth. Anything they can see of your contact details goes through the member directory, which follows your privacy settings and never shows a minor's details.

## Why we collect it

- To run your membership and your branch.
- To verify who you are and keep the community safe.
- To send you the notices and receipts you ask for.
- To record payments and issue receipts.
- To meet legal and accounting duties.

We do not sell your data and we do not show advertising.

## Where your data is stored

- Member data is stored in India, in a Supabase database in Mumbai.
- Files such as photos and documents are stored in India, on DigitalOcean Spaces in Bangalore.
- Backups are encrypted and kept for at most 6 months.

### Services that process limited data outside India

- **Meta (WhatsApp):** phone numbers and message text, when we send you a WhatsApp message.
- **Expo and Google (push notifications):** device tokens and notification text.
- **Sentry (crash reports):** crash traces that contain no personal data. No analytics or crash user details are collected for minors.
- **GitHub Actions:** the backup job passes an already encrypted copy of the database through its servers. The copy cannot be read there.
- **Our email provider:** your email address and the message text. [TO BE PROVIDED BY AIAIA: the email provider, and whether mail is processed in India.]

## What messages contain

- Push and WhatsApp messages never contain phone numbers, dates of birth or payment amounts.
- Receipt emails sent to the payer do show the amount. Receipt push and WhatsApp messages carry only the receipt number.
- Safety alerts to a child's guardian carry the child's first name, the event, the venue and the time.

## How long we keep it

- Rejected applications and deleted accounts are kept for 6 months, then permanently erased. Your private details are deleted, your profile is anonymised and your files are removed.
- Financial records such as payments and receipts are kept for the period our accountant requires, without your contact details. [TO BE PROVIDED BY AIAIA: retention period for financial records.]
- Backups are kept for at most 6 months.

## Your rights

You may ask to see, correct, download or delete your data, to withdraw your consent, and to complain. Most of this can be done in the app under Settings. For anything else, contact us (see Contact Us).

## Children and guardians

- A person under 18 needs a parent or guardian. The guardian gives consent and can manage the child's membership while their link to the child is active.
- Guardians must be 18 or older. The link ends automatically on the child's 18th birthday, and from then on the member controls their own account and is asked for their own consent.
- A minor's phone number and date of birth are never shown to other members. AIAIA officers can see them, as they can for every member, because they are responsible for members' safety and membership.
- Public pages show only a minor's first name and last initial.
- Guardians receive an alert when their child is checked in at an event.

## WhatsApp and email

We send WhatsApp and email messages about your membership, events and receipts. You can switch non-essential messages off in Settings. Some messages, such as receipts and safety alerts, are sent regardless.

## Payments

Payments are processed by Razorpay. Razorpay receives what it needs to take the payment and gives us the result. See Razorpay's own privacy policy for how it handles your payment details.
