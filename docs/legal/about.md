# About AIAIA

AIAIA is a community organisation, and the AIAIA Youth App is how its members, officers and branches stay connected. This page is a placeholder draft.

## What the app does

- Lets members join their branch and keep their profile and digital ID in one place.
- Shares announcements and events from the national and branch teams.
- Handles dues, event fees and donations, with receipts.
- Keeps a private gallery and documents library for members.

## Who it is for

- Youth wing: members under 35.
- AIAIA: members aged 35 and above.
- Dependents: children and young people managed by a parent or guardian until they turn 18.

## Our commitments

- Member data is kept in India, and is visible only to the people who need it.
- Children's data is protected. A minor's phone number and date of birth are never shown to other members; only AIAIA officers and the child's guardian can see them.
- The app is open source. The code is public, and member data never is.
