# Delete Your Account

You can delete your AIAIA Youth App account yourself. This page explains how, and what happens to your data.

## How to delete your account

1. Choose "Sign in to delete" below, and sign in with your phone number.
2. Open Settings, then Delete account.
3. Confirm. Your account is closed straight away.

You do not need to install the app. The same steps work on the web.

## What gets deleted

- You are signed out everywhere and can no longer use the app.
- Your profile is hidden from other members.
- Your phone number, date of birth, email address and emergency contact are deleted.
- Your photo and uploaded files are deleted.
- Your push notification tokens are deleted.

## What we keep, and for how long

- For 6 months after you delete your account we keep your record, so that an accidental or disputed deletion can be put right. After 6 months it is permanently erased: your private details are deleted, your profile is anonymised and your files are removed.
- Backups that hold your data are replaced within 6 months.
- Payment and receipt records are kept for the period AIAIA's accountant requires, without your contact details.

## If you are a guardian

Deleting your account ends your access to your dependents' accounts. Their accounts are not deleted. Ask the branch to arrange a new guardian.

## If you cannot sign in

Contact us (see Contact Us) from the phone number or email on your account, and we will delete it for you. [TO BE PROVIDED BY AIAIA: the support email address.]
