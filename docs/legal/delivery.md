This policy is a placeholder draft, pending approval by AIAIA.

## What you receive

AIAIA services are digital. When a payment succeeds you receive the following.

- **Dues:** your membership is shown as paid for the period, and a receipt is issued.
- **Event fees:** your place at the event and your entry pass appear in the app.
- **Donations:** a receipt is issued.
- **Certificates:** issued in the app by an officer, when eligible.
